Review guide

  1. CompareRead the summary against the transcript.
  2. Add findingsHighlight text, add a note and link turns.
  3. FinishChoose a decision. The next unfinished call opens.

Use the transcript as your reference. Some summaries are accurate. Flag omissions only for discussed information required by the rules.

Meaningful errors

Wrong amounts, dates, payment status, authority, hardship or next steps can mislead the next colleague. Cite the relevant turns.

Keep nuance

Deferral is not refusal. An estimate is not a promise. Contact holds do not cancel debt. Mark uncertainty when evidence is ambiguous.

Rule exceptions

Full or confirmed pre-call security is required for “DPA completed”. Otherwise use “no DPA stated with agent”. Retain named advice bodies; redact identifying private entities.

Amounts depend on context

Omit numerical arrears, balances and payments taken now. Preserve relevant situational costs and exact future payment commitments.
